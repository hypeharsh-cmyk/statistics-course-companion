## Questions

### Q1. Normal Distribution --- Classification of Students (5 marks)

In an examination it is laid down that a student passes if he secures 30
percent or more marks. He is placed in the first, second or third
division according as he secures 60% or more marks, between 45% and 60%
marks, and marks between 30% and 45% respectively. He gets distinction
in case he secures 80% or more marks. It is noticed from the result that
10% of the students failed in the examination, whereas 5% of them
obtained distinction. Calculate the percentage of students placed in the
second division. (Assume the normal distribution of marks). **(5)**

### Q2. Chebyshev's Inequality --- Fair Die (5 marks)

If (X) is the random variable denoting the number scored in a throw of a
fair die, show that the Chebyshev's inequality gives

\[ P{\|X-`\mu`{=tex}\|\>2.5}\<0.47, \]

where (`\mu`{=tex}) is the mean of (X). **(5)**

### Q3. Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean (2+2+2 marks)

According to a survey, high school students average 100 text messages
daily. Assume the population standard deviation is 20 text messages.
Suppose a random sample of 50 students are taken.

**a.** What is the probability that the sample mean is more than 105?

**b.** What is the probability that the sample mean is less than 95?

**c.** What is the probability that the sample mean is between 90 and
115? **(2+2+2)**

### Q4. 95% Confidence Interval for (`\mu`{=tex}) (6 marks)

Let ({1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9}) be the set of collected random sample
values from a (N(`\mu`{=tex},`\sigma`{=tex}\^2)) distribution, where
both (`\mu`{=tex}) and (`\sigma`{=tex}\^2) are unknown. Find the 95%
confidence interval for (`\mu`{=tex}). **(6)**

### Q5. Hypothesis Test for a Population Proportion (7 marks)

A manufacturer claimed that at least 90% of the components which he
supplied are conforming to the specifications. However, a random sample
of 200 components showed only 164 were conforming to the specifications.
Test the claim of the manufacturer at 1% level of significance by using
p-value method. **(7)**

### Q6. Hypothesis Test for Population Variance (6 marks)

Weights (in kgs) of 10 students are given below:

**38, 40, 45, 53, 47, 43, 55, 48, 52, 49.**

Can we say that the population variance of distribution of weights of
all students from which the above sample of 10 students was drawn, is
equal to 20 square kgs. Test an appropriate hypothesis at 5% level.
**(6)**

### Q8. Regression and Correlation (2+2+2 marks)

In a partially destroyed laboratory record of an analysis of bivariate
data the following results only legible.

\[ `\operatorname{Var}`{=tex}(X)=9. \]

Two regression lines are

\[ 8X-10Y+66=0 \]

and

\[ 40X-18Y=214. \]

Find:

1.  The mean values of (X) and (Y). **(2+2)**
2.  The coefficient of correlation between (X) and (Y). **(2)**
3.  The standard deviation of (Y). **(2)**
